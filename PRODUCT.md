# Product Brief: AgroSphere Pro

## Vision & Core Value
AgroSphere Pro is a modern, unified agricultural ecosystem platform designed to empower farmers, agribusinesses, and commodity buyers. It combines real-time farm and crop monitoring, transparent marketplace trade, grain-to-shelf traceability, intelligent financial forecasting, and a community knowledge network into a high-performance web experience.

## Target Audience
- **Primary**: Progressive and commercial farmers managing parcels, crops, input inventory, and farm finances.
- **Secondary**: Agricultural produce buyers, distributors, and logistics partners seeking verified batch provenance and direct farmer trading.
- **Tertiary**: Agricultural extension officers and community moderators sharing advisory practices and subsidy schemes.

## Core Functional Capabilities
1. **Command Dashboard**: Real-time overview of active farms, sowing cycles, marketplace revenue, and critical task alerts.
2. **Farm & Land Intelligence**: Multi-farm acreage registry, seasonal crop lifecycles (Rabi/Kharif), and input inventory telemetry (seeds, fertilizers, bio-nutrients).
3. **Agri-Marketplace & Orders**: Direct listing engine with price-per-unit discovery, dynamic shopping cart/checkout, automated batch generation, and delivery tracking.
4. **Batch Provenance & Traceability**: Cryptographic batch verification (`AGRO-YYYYMMDD-XXXX`), farm origin authentication, timeline dispatch tracking, and buyer verification views.
5. **Smart Farm Finance**: Double-entry income vs. expense ledger, net margin analytics, Mandi price projection models with trend signals, and national subsidy application guides.
6. **Agri-Community Forum**: Verified knowledge exchange forum with discussion threads, answers, upvotes, and category filtering.
7. **Identity & Roles**: Role-based access control (`Farmer`, `Buyer`, `Admin`) with secure session state.
